# Encrypto Credit Line

<span class="badge badge--soon">Coming Soon</span>

A revolving credit line that doesn't require collateral. Borrow, spend, repay — like a traditional credit card, but powered by on-chain underwriting.

## How It's Different

Traditional credit cards require a FICO score, a Social Security number, and a US address. That excludes most of the world. Encrypto's credit line is underwritten using on-chain data and account behavior — accessible to anyone with a demonstrated financial history on-chain.

## Underwriting Model

Encrypto's credit engine evaluates a composite score based on:

### On-Chain Signals
- **Wallet age and activity.** How long has the wallet been active? Consistent activity signals reliability.
- **Asset diversity.** A diversified portfolio suggests financial sophistication.
- **DeFi history.** Previous lending/borrowing behavior, repayment track record, no liquidation history.
- **Transaction volume.** Regular, non-wash-trade transaction patterns.

### Encrypto Account Signals
- **Account age.** How long the user has been on Encrypto.
- **Spending history.** Consistent card usage and repayment.
- **Points tier.** Higher-tier users (more engagement) qualify for higher limits.
- **KYC verification level.** Full verification unlocks higher limits.

### External Signals (Optional)
- **Traditional credit bureau.** For users who opt in, we can factor in traditional credit data to boost their limit.
- **Employment verification.** Optional, can increase credit line.

## Credit Line Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Starting limit** | $500 - $5,000 |
| **Max limit** | $50,000 (tier-dependent) |
| **Interest rate** | 12-24% APR (score-dependent) |
| **Min payment** | 2% of balance or $25, whichever is greater |
| **Payment cycle** | Monthly |
| **Grace period** | 25 days (no interest on new purchases if paid in full) |
| **Late fee** | $15 |

## How It Works

1. **Apply.** Opt in from your Encrypto dashboard. No paperwork — underwriting is automatic.
2. **Get approved.** Credit line amount is determined by your composite score. Instant decision.
3. **Spend.** Your Encrypto Card now has a credit component. Spend up to your credit limit even with a $0 USDC balance.
4. **Statement.** Monthly statement with all credit transactions, interest charges, and minimum payment.
5. **Repay.** Pay from your USDC balance, bank transfer, or any supported crypto.

## Why This Matters for LATAM

In Latin America, credit card penetration is under 30% in most countries. Millions of people with stable income and spending capacity can't access credit because traditional scoring infrastructure doesn't cover them.

Encrypto's on-chain underwriting model doesn't care where you live, what language you speak, or whether your country has a credit bureau. If you have demonstrable on-chain financial activity, you can access credit. That's a fundamentally different approach to financial inclusion.
