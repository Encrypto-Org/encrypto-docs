# Credit & Lending

<span class="badge badge--soon">Coming Soon</span>

Encrypto is building lending products that let users borrow against their crypto holdings or access credit lines based on their on-chain activity.

## The Vision

Your crypto portfolio is an asset. It should work for you the same way a stock portfolio does at a traditional brokerage — as collateral for borrowing, as the basis for a credit line, as a way to access liquidity without selling.

Today, if you need cash, your options are: sell crypto (tax event, lose your position), use DeFi lending (complex, liquidation risk), or get a traditional loan (doesn't recognize crypto as collateral). Encrypto's lending products will solve all three problems.

## On-Chain Underwriting

Traditional credit scoring excludes billions of people — especially in emerging markets where formal credit infrastructure doesn't exist. On-chain data provides a richer, more accurate picture of creditworthiness: wallet activity, asset diversity, transaction history, and DeFi behavior create signals that traditional bureaus can't capture.

This is what makes crypto-native lending fundamentally different. We're building a system that can underwrite people the old system never could.
