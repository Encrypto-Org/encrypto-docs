# Credit & Lending

<span class="badge badge--soon">Coming Soon</span>

Encrypto is building a full-stack lending platform that lets users borrow against their crypto holdings or access credit lines based on their on-chain activity. This isn't DeFi lending with liquidation bots — it's structured credit designed for real people.

## The Vision

Your crypto portfolio is an asset. It should work for you the same way a stock portfolio does at a traditional brokerage — as collateral for margin, as the basis for a credit line, as a way to access liquidity without selling.

Today, if you need $5,000 for an emergency, your options are:

1. **Sell crypto.** Creates a tax event, you lose your position, and you miss the upside.
2. **DeFi lending.** Complex, risky, liquidation thresholds that can wipe you out in a volatile market.
3. **Traditional loan.** Doesn't recognize crypto as collateral. Slow, paperwork-heavy.

Encrypto's lending products solve all three problems by offering both collateralized and uncollateralized borrowing with clear terms, no hidden liquidation mechanics, and direct integration with your Encrypto Card.

## Why On-Chain Credit Is Better

Traditional credit scoring is broken. It's based on a narrow set of signals (payment history, credit utilization, length of history) that systematically exclude billions of people — especially in emerging markets where formal credit infrastructure doesn't exist.

On-chain data provides a richer, more accurate picture of creditworthiness:

| Signal | Traditional | On-Chain |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **Income verification** | Pay stubs, tax returns | Wallet inflows, DeFi yields, staking rewards |
| **Asset verification** | Bank statements | Real-time portfolio valuation |
| **Payment history** | Credit bureau reports | On-chain loan repayment history |
| **Risk behavior** | Limited signals | Leverage ratios, liquidation history, protocol usage |
| **Geographic coverage** | Country-dependent | Global, permissionless |

This is what makes crypto-native lending fundamentally different. We're not just building a faster version of the old system — we're building a system that can underwrite people the old system never could.
