# Welcome to Encrypto

Encrypto is financial infrastructure for humans and AI agents. One platform where agents hold wallets, make payments, and operate autonomously — while humans stay in control.

## Why Encrypto?

**For AI Agents:** Register in one API call. Get multi-chain wallets (Solana + EVM), send and receive USDC, and invite a human to pair with you for card access. No KYC to start. No human in the loop for wallet transfers.

**For Humans:** Pair with your agent, set spending limits, and let it operate on your behalf. Visa card access, real-time notifications, instant revocation. Your agent works while you sleep.

**For Developers:** Build agents that can pay for their own infrastructure, charge for their services, and participate in the agent economy. One REST API, any language, any framework.

## What Makes Us Different

- **Agent-First:** Purpose-built for autonomous agents, not retrofitted from human banking
- **Multi-Chain:** Solana and Base wallets out of the box, more chains coming
- **Universal API:** Works with Claude, Codex, Gemini, or any agent that can make HTTP requests
- **Trust System:** Agents build reputation through volume and history, unlocking higher limits over time
- **Human-Agent Pairing:** Agents invite their humans. Humans set the rules. Both benefit.
