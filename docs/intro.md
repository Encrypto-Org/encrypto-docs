# Encrypto

**The Sovereign Liquidity Layer for the global economy.**

Encrypto is a crypto-native neobank that eliminates the friction between holding digital assets and spending them in the real world. We abstract away the complexity of bridging, swapping, and off-ramping — so users can hold any crypto and spend it instantly, anywhere Visa is accepted.

Traditional finance built payments infrastructure for a world of fiat currency. That infrastructure is slow, expensive, and excludes billions of people. Crypto solved the money problem but created a usability problem — you can hold a portfolio worth six figures and still can't buy coffee with it.

Encrypto solves this by sitting at the intersection of on-chain liquidity and off-chain payments. Our Liquidity Engine handles real-time asset conversion, slippage optimization, and settlement — so end users just see a card that works.

## Why Crypto-Native

Most fintech products are built on legacy banking rails and bolt crypto on as an afterthought. Encrypto is built the other way around — crypto-native infrastructure with fiat as the output layer.

This matters because:

- **Settlement is faster.** On-chain settlement is final in seconds, not days. No chargebacks, no holdbacks, no three-day ACH delays.
- **Liquidity is global.** We tap into on-chain DEX liquidity across multiple chains, not a single market maker's order book.
- **Custody is non-custodial by default.** Users hold their own keys through embedded wallets. We never take custody of user funds.
- **The unit economics are better.** No interchange middlemen, no correspondent banking fees, no nostro/vostro account management. Just clean on-chain settlement.

## Who This Is For

- **Crypto holders** who want to actually use their assets without off-ramping to a bank account and waiting three days.
- **People in Latin America** and emerging markets who are underserved by traditional banking and deal with currency volatility daily.
- **Anyone** who wants a financial account that works globally from day one, without geographic restrictions or minimum balances.
