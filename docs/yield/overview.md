# Yield

<span class="badge badge--soon">Coming Soon</span>

Your money should work for you. Encrypto integrates yield directly into the wallet experience — not as a separate DeFi feature, but as a core part of how your account works. Think of it like a high-interest checking account, but powered by crypto infrastructure.

## How It Works

Every Encrypto account earns yield by default. When you hold a balance, it's not sitting idle — it's earning. No opt-in required, no separate "savings" account, no lockups.

```
Your Balance: $1,000 USDC
├── Available to Spend: $1,000 (always liquid)
└── Earning: 4.2% APY (accrues daily)
```

Yield accrues on your entire balance automatically. You can spend, send, or withdraw at any time — there's no penalty for moving your money. It's your money, and it's always available.

## Risk Levels

Not all users want the same risk profile. Encrypto offers three tiers:

| Tier | Risk | Target APY | How It Works |
|------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **Standard** | Low | 3-5% | Default for all accounts. Conservative lending and stablecoin strategies. |
| **Enhanced** | Medium | 5-10% | Opt-in. LP positions in stable pairs, restaking strategies. |
| **Aggressive** | Higher | 10%+ | Opt-in. Concentrated liquidity, leveraged strategies. Higher returns, more volatility. |

Every account starts on **Standard** — earning yield with minimal risk. Users who want higher returns can opt into Enhanced or Aggressive tiers from the dashboard. You can change tiers at any time.

## Why This Is Different

### vs. Traditional Banking

Traditional savings accounts in the US offer 0.01-0.5% APY. Even "high-yield" savings accounts top out around 4-5%. In emerging markets, real returns on savings are often negative after inflation.

Encrypto's yield comes from on-chain lending markets, liquidity provision, and staking — sources of return that don't exist in traditional banking. Crypto infrastructure enables yield that banks structurally can't offer.

### vs. DeFi

DeFi yield opportunities exist — lending markets, liquidity provision, staking, restaking — but accessing them requires:

1. Knowing which protocols are safe (and which are not)
2. Understanding impermanent loss, liquidation risk, smart contract risk
3. Managing positions across multiple protocols and chains
4. Monitoring rates and rebalancing when yields shift

This is too complex for most people. Encrypto abstracts all of it. The Yield Engine handles protocol selection, position management, and rebalancing behind the scenes. Users just see a number that goes up.

## Key Properties

- **Always liquid.** Your full balance is available for spending, sending, or withdrawing at any time. No lockups, no unbonding periods.
- **Auto-compound.** Yield is automatically reinvested unless the user opts out.
- **Transparent.** Users can see exactly which strategies their funds are deployed to and the current APY.
- **Audited.** Only protocols that have passed independent security audits are eligible for inclusion.
- **Default on.** You don't need to "activate" yield. It's part of how the account works.
