# Encrypto Card

<span class="badge badge--live">Live</span>

The Encrypto Card is a Visa debit card backed by your crypto balance. It works anywhere Visa is accepted — online, in-store, Apple Pay, Google Pay, contactless. No difference from any other card, except the funding source is your on-chain assets instead of a bank account.

## The Problem

You can hold $100,000 in crypto and still can't pay for an Uber. The off-ramp experience today looks like this:

1. Sell crypto on an exchange (fees, slippage, tax event)
2. Withdraw to a bank account (1-3 business days, wire fees)
3. Wait for it to clear (another 1-2 days)
4. Then spend it

That's a 3-5 day process with multiple friction points, fees, and tax implications just to buy a coffee.

## The Solution

Encrypto eliminates every step. Your card is linked directly to your on-chain balance. When you tap your card, the transaction amount is settled from your USDC balance in real time. No pre-conversion, no bank intermediary, no delays.

```
Card tap → Authorization → USDC debit → Visa settlement → Merchant paid
         ~200ms              instant         same day
```

## Key Features

- **Instant activation.** Card is live the moment KYC is approved. No waiting for physical delivery.
- **Virtual + Physical.** Use the virtual card immediately. Physical card ships to your address.
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay.** Add to your mobile wallet for contactless payments.
- **Global acceptance.** Anywhere Visa is accepted, in any currency. FX conversion is handled automatically.
- **Real-time balance.** Every transaction reflects instantly. No pending charges sitting in limbo.
- **No overdraft.** You can't spend more than you have. The card declines if your balance is insufficient.
